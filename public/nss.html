Foundation & Purpose

About NSS at TGPCOP

The National Service Scheme was established in 1969 to mark the birth centenary of Mahatma Gandhi. At Tulsiramji Gaikwad-Patil College of Pharmacy, our unit integrates pharmaceutical knowledge with direct community welfare — instilling social responsibility, empathy, and civic leadership in future healthcare professionals.

🤝

Community Service

Organizing grassroots community assistance, rural health awareness, and door-to-door welfare surveys in neighboring villages across Vidarbha.

🏥

Health Awareness

Promoting rational medicine usage, patient counseling on generic pharmaceuticals, antibiotic resistance education, and preventive health screenings.

🌱

Environmental Responsibility

Active participation in tree plantation campaigns, plastic-free campus drives, and village cleanliness under the nationwide Swachh Bharat Abhiyan.
