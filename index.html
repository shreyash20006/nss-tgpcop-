FOUNDATION & PURPOSE

About NSS at TGPCOP

The National Service Scheme was formally inaugurated in 1969 to mark the birth centenary of Mahatma Gandhi. At Tulsiramji Gaikwad-Patil College of Pharmacy, our unit integrates pharmaceutical knowledge with direct civic service — instilling social responsibility, empathy, and professional integrity in future healthcare professionals.

🏥

Public Health Literacy

Promoting rational drug use, preventive health awareness, patient medication compliance, and generic pharmaceutical counseling in local semi-urban and rural communities.

🌱

Environmental Action

Tree plantation drives, campus energy audits, anti-plastic campaigns, and comprehensive village sanitation drives under the Swachh Bharat Abhiyan.

🎓

Civic Leadership & Teamwork

Instilling ethical teamwork, civic courage, disaster management readiness, and public speaking skills, equipping pharmacy students to become empathetic leaders in India's healthcare landscape.

COMMUNITY INITIATIVES

NSS Service Domains

Structured avenues of community engagement where pharmacy students step forward to make a direct positive difference in Vidarbha's rural and semi-urban localities.

🩸

Blood Donation & Diagnostic Outreach

Voluntary blood donation coordination, free blood pressure and glucose screenings, and public health awareness drives organized in collaboration with accredited medical centers and blood banks.

Health Initiative Community Care
🌳

Van Mahotsav & Eco-Action

Plantation of native and medicinal saplings across campus grounds, neighboring rural schools, and adopted villages, promoting environmental stewardship and student tree adoption.

Green Campus Environmental Protection
🧹

Swachh Bharat Cleanliness Drives

Community shramdaan sanitation drives, public park cleanliness, plastic abatement campaigns, and community demonstrations on the safe disposal of expired and unused pharmaceuticals.

Social Cleanliness Public Hygiene
⛺

Rural Development & Special Camps

Flagship residential community service programs where student volunteers reside in adopted villages, conducting door-to-door wellness surveys, educational sessions, and community outreach.

Flagship Program Rural Outreach
