About NSS at TGPCOP

The National Service Scheme (NSS) at Tulsiramji Gaikwad-Patil College of Pharmacy provides students with opportunities to engage in meaningful nation-building activities, personality development through community service, and grassroots healthcare outreach.

🤝

Community Service

Organizing free rural medical health checkups, blood donation camps, cleanliness drives under Swachh Bharat Abhiyan, and tree plantation initiatives across neighboring adopted villages.

🎓

Leadership Development

Instilling democratic leadership, team-building, civic responsibility, and emergency response skills that prepare healthcare professionals to act as community pillars.

📢

Social Awareness

Promoting awareness on pharmaceutical literacy, antibiotic misuse, organ donation, menstrual health, environmental conservation, and social equity through street plays and public rallies.

How to Register as an NSS Volunteer

Follow our simple 4-step registration process to receive your official digital membership credentials.

1

Submit Application

Fill out the online enrollment form with your college roll number, contact details, and a clear passport photo.

2

Coordinator Review

The NSS Program Officer reviews your enrollment and verifies your student credentials in the institutional database.

3

Get Digital ID Card

Upon approval, you receive a unique Volunteer ID and an encrypted QR-coded digital identification card.

4

Participate & Earn

Attend workshops, social drives, and special 7-day residential camps to earn accredited service certificates.
