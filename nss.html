FOUNDATION & PURPOSE

About NSS at TGPCOP

The National Service Scheme was launched in 1969 to foster personality development through community service. At Tulsiramji Gaikwad-Patil College of Pharmacy, our unit bridges academic pharmaceutical knowledge with genuine grassroots service.

🏥

Public Health Outreach

Bridging healthcare gaps by organizing rural health screenings, blood donation drives, pharmaceutical counseling on generic medicines, and antibiotic resistance awareness in neighboring communities.

🌱

Environmental Action

Pioneering tree plantation drives ("Ek Ped Maa Ke Naam"), campus energy audits, anti-plastic campaigns, and comprehensive village sanitation drives under the Swachh Bharat Abhiyan.

🎓

Leadership & Citizenship

Instilling ethical teamwork, civic courage, disaster management readiness, and public speaking skills, equipping students to become empathetic leaders in India's healthcare landscape.

TK
Healthcare education is truly tested not inside air-conditioned classrooms, but where medical care is scarce. NSS gives our pharmacy students the courage and empathy to serve rural families, organize emergency camps, and stand up as leaders for India's future.
Tushar Kalbhut
President — Student Council & NSS Unit
B.Pharm Final Year • Tulsiramji Gaikwad-Patil College of Pharmacy
COMMUNITY PROGRAMMES

Key Activities & Initiatives

Hands-on social engagement where pharmacy students step forward to make a direct positive difference in Vidarbha's rural and semi-urban pockets.

🩸

Blood Donation & Diagnostic Camps

Organized in active partnership with recognized district blood banks. Volunteers coordinate voluntary blood donation, free blood pressure/sugar checks, and hemoglobin screenings for college and local residents.

Annual Event 500+ Donors
🌳

Van Mahotsav & Eco-Action

Plantation of indigenous and medicinal saplings (Neem, Tulsi, Ashoka) across campus boundaries, local rural schools, and adopted villages, followed by student adoption for sustained nurture.

Green Campus 1,200+ Trees
🧹

Swachh Bharat Cleanliness Drives

Regular shramdaan drives in Mohgaon village, public park sanitization, garbage segregation workshops, and awareness sessions demonstrating safe disposal of expired medicines.

Social Cleanliness Bi-Monthly
⛺

7-Day Annual Rural Special Camp

The hallmark NSS residential experience where volunteers reside for one full week in an adopted rural village, conducting doorstep health surveys, educational sessions, and community sports.

Flagship Program Adopted Village
